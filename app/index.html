How to read the confidence notes

Names, descriptions, grades and the trophy count follow the frozen PS5 list in the kit. The app labels time-sensitive or unverified claims instead of silently turning them into requirements. A manual counter is a personal note only; it does not read the console trophy service.

Local saves, import and spoiler settings

Checklist ticks, optional counters and the spoiler setting are stored in this browser only. Export before changing devices or clearing browser data.

Import Merge combines earned names and keeps the larger value for each counter. Replace replaces this browser's tracker. A bad or unsupported file is rejected without changing your save.

The story switch hides unearned story names, descriptions and guidance in the checklist, search results and roadmap suggestions. The small H mark means PlayStation hides that trophy's name until earned; it is a separate flag.
